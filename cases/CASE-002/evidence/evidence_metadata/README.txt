CASE-002 — ALL EVIDENCE IN ONE FOLDER

This package keeps all public-source evidence/media references for the Mundra Port case together.

Evidence hierarchy:
- Official NIA/DRI/PIB/MHA documents = primary sources.
- Court judgment = court record.
- News reports/videos = supplementary OSINT.
- Photo/video entries are references where the publisher owns/hosts the media; do not assume every image is an evidentiary photograph.
- Do not convert allegations into convictions.
- Do not add private victim information, bank details, phone numbers, addresses, or leaked personal data.

The DRI 2021-22 report is particularly valuable because its Mundra case section contains photographs of seized material/packaging.
